Salted Caramel Brownies

by Daniel

A rich fudgy chocolate brownie with a shiny, slightly crackly top Drizzled with warm, golden salted caramel sauce and sprinkles with course flaky sea salt. Best served warm.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup unsalted butter, melted
  • 2 cups granulated sugar
  • 4 large eggs, at room temperature
  • 1 tablespoon vanilla extract
  • 1 cup all-purpose flour
  • 1 cup unsweetened cocoa powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1/2 cup salted caramel sauce (store-bought or homemade)
  • 1 teaspoon flaky sea salt (for topping)

Directions

  1. Preheat oven to 350° F (175° C).
  2. Line an 8x8-inch baking pan with parchment paper, leaving an overhang on two sides.
  3. Combine Wet Ingredients: In a large bowl, whisk the melted butter and sugar together until smooth. Add the eggs one at a time, whisking well after each, then stir in the vanilla extract.
  4. Fold Dry Ingredients: Sift in the flour, cocoa powder, and salt. Gently fold with a spatula until just combined (do not overmix).
  5. Layer & Swirl: Pour half of the brownie batter into the prepared pan. Drizzle half of the caramel sauce over it. Add the remaining batter, then drizzle the rest of the caramel over the top. Use a knife to gently swirl the caramel into the batter.
  6. Bake & Finish: Bake for 30–35 minutes, or until a toothpick inserted near the center comes out with a few moist crumbs. Sprinkle immediately with flaky sea salt. Allow to cool completely in the pan before slicing.
